# ResilAI — Business Model & Strategy

## 💼 Business Model Summary

ResilAI operates as a **B2B SaaS and Managed Service Provider (MSP) Enablement Platform** targeting Healthcare Networks and Critical Care Clinics.

---

## 💵 Pricing & Packaging Tiers

1. **Clinic Starter Tier**
   * **Target**: Single independent clinics or dental offices.
   * **Features**: M365 + Veeam connectors, continuous readiness scoring, automated Monday Morning reports.
   * **Pricing**: $299 / month per clinic location.

2. **MSP Partner Tier (Multi-Tenant)**
   * **Target**: Managed Service Providers managing 10+ healthcare sites.
   * **Features**: Multi-tenant dashboard, unified risk ledger, white-labeled executive reports, Wazuh + Splunk integrations.
   * **Pricing**: $199 / clinic / month (minimum 10 clinics).

3. **Healthcare Enterprise Network**
   * **Target**: Regional hospital systems and health networks (50+ locations).
   * **Features**: Custom telemetry connectors, dedicated GCP tenant isolation, sovereign data embassy compliance, 24/7 SLA.
   * **Pricing**: Custom annual contract ($50k - $250k / year).
