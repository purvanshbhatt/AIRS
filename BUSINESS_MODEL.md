# ResilAI — Business Model & Strategy

## 💼 Business Model Summary

ResilAI is a **B2B SaaS** product sold by monthly subscription to small healthcare clinics, law firms and the managed service providers (MSPs) that run their IT.

Status: pre-revenue. No paying customers and no signed pilots yet.

---

## 💵 Pricing & Packaging Tiers

The pricing page is the source of truth: https://resilai.org/pricing (machine-readable copy: `frontend/public/pricing.md`). If this file and the pricing page differ, the pricing page is right.

1. **Founding Sysadmin / Design Partner**
   * **Target**: One-person IT teams at clinics and law firms.
   * **Pricing**: $149 / month, locked for life for the first 50 customers (regular price $199 / month).
   * **Limits**: Up to 250 network nodes and endpoints, 1 organization, 1 administrator.

2. **Growth**
   * **Target**: Larger practices and IT providers with more than one administrator.
   * **Pricing**: $399 / month (regular price $499 / month).
   * **Limits**: Up to 500 network nodes and endpoints, up to 5 administrators.

3. **Enterprise**
   * **Target**: Multi-site organizations that need a private deployment or single sign-on.
   * **Pricing**: Custom contract. No price is published.

4. **Free demo**
   * **Pricing**: $0. Simulated data only; no live connectors.
