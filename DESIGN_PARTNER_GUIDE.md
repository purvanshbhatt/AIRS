## 🎯 Program Goals

* **Deployment Duration**: 60-day pilot program.
* **Scope**: Integration with existing M365 tenant, Veeam backup server, and EDR agents.
* **Key Deliverables**: 
  1. Automated weekly Monday Morning readiness briefing for clinic leadership.
  2. Zero-touch continuous evidence collection.
  3. Tailored resilience recommendations for healthcare workflows.
