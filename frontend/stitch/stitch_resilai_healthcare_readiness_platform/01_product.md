# ResilAI: Product Overview

## What is ResilAI?
ResilAI is an automated, continuous security and compliance verification platform. It removes the guesswork from IT operations by continuously asserting the state of critical infrastructure, integrations, and readiness, turning what used to be manual audits into a real-time, deterministic "yes" or "no" answer.

## Target Customer
Our primary customers are **Healthcare Clinics**. Healthcare clinics handle highly sensitive Protected Health Information (PHI) and operate under strict compliance frameworks (like HIPAA). They often have lean IT teams or rely on MSPs, meaning the clinic executives and managing partners need immediate, plain-English assurance that their patient data is safe and their systems are operational without needing to decipher technical logs.

## The Core Question
The entire product experience is designed to answer a single, critical question every morning:
> **"Are we ready for today?"**

When a clinic executive opens the app on their phone or desktop, this is the first thing they must see. They don't want a dashboard of 50 complex charts; they want a definitive statement on whether their digital doors can safely open.

## Executive vs. IT User

### The Executive (Managing Partner / Medical Director)
- **Mindset**: "Is my patient data safe? Can we operate today? What is my liability?"
- **Needs**: The "Morning Brief." A simple, mobile-first view answering if they are ready for the day. Clear red/green status. A list of items needing immediate attention. 
- **Time spent**: Less than 2 minutes a day.

### The IT User (MSP / Internal IT Admin)
- **Mindset**: "How do I fix what's broken? Are my integrations healthy? Where is the compliance drift?"
- **Needs**: The "Operations Center." Deep-dive technical telemetry, API connector health, detailed forensic logs, remediation ledgers, and raw data on system state. 
- **Time spent**: Deep work, debugging, and configuration.

## What Makes Us Different?
1. **Deterministic, Not Probabilistic**: We don't guess based on anomalies; we assert truths. (e.g., "MFA is ON," "Backup succeeded at 2 AM").
2. **Actionable Executive Translation**: We translate complex technical posture directly into business readiness and liability risk. 
3. **Continuous Verification**: Audits aren't a once-a-year event; they happen every night.
4. **Healthcare-First Compliance**: Built specifically for the high-stakes, low-margin-for-error environment of modern medical clinics.
