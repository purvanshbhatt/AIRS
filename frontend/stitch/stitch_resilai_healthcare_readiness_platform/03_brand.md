# ResilAI: Brand & Aesthetic Identity

## Logo Colors
*   **Primary Accent:** Vibrant, trust-inspiring colors (e.g., modern deep blue or emerald green) that signify "Ready" and "Secure".
*   **Status Colors:** Clear, unmistakable semantic colors. 
    *   `Green` = Ready / Verified.
    *   `Red` / `Orange` = Action Required / Drift Detected.
*   **Backgrounds:** Deep, rich tones for dark mode; crisp, clinical whites and soft slates for light mode.

## Typography
*   Clean, modern, highly legible sans-serif fonts (e.g., Inter, SF Pro, or Roboto).
*   Bold, authoritative headers that convey certainty.
*   High contrast text to ensure readability for busy executives quickly glancing at their screens.

## Style & Inspiration
*   **Apple Health:** For its ability to take massive amounts of complex biological data and present it in a simple, highly visual, and easily digestible daily summary.
*   **Stripe:** For its premium, developer-centric but executive-friendly aesthetic, subtle micro-animations, and perfect spacing.
*   **Linear:** For its ultra-fast, modern, opinionated interface and dark-mode excellence.
*   **Mercury:** For its high-end, trustworthy financial aesthetic that appeals to executives and decision-makers.
*   **Calm:** For a user experience that reduces anxiety—turning the typically stressful domain of IT security into a reassuring daily check-in.

## Key Adjectives
*   **Premium:** The interface should feel expensive and highly polished.
*   **Healthcare:** It must look appropriate for a medical setting without feeling outdated or bureaucratic.
*   **Executive:** The primary view is for leadership; it must command respect and authority.
*   **Dark Mode:** A flawless, rich dark mode is essential for modern premium applications.
