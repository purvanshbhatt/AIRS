# ResilAI Frontend Rules

These rules must be followed by all design and engineering teams (including AI agents like Stitch, OpenCode, and Antigravity) when modifying or generating frontend components.

## 1. Product Experience Rules
- **Never generate generic cybersecurity SaaS dashboards.** The product must feel unique to the ResilAI brand.
- **Executive Dashboard First:** The primary view is built for clinic leadership. It must answer one question immediately: *"Are we ready today?"*
- **Technical Workspace Separate:** IT details, network logs, and deep API metrics belong exclusively in the Operations Center. Do not clutter the executive view.
- **Healthcare First:** The aesthetic and data models must respect the constraints and severity of a clinical environment.

## 2. Brand Guidelines
- **Use existing logo colors:** Adhere strictly to the defined primary and status colors (Green for Ready, Red/Orange for Drift).
- **Preserve the ResilAI identity:** Maintain a consistent tone of authority, clarity, and security.
- **Storytelling:** Approach data visualization with an "Apple Health" style narrative. Take complex telemetry and present it as a simple, digestible daily summary.
